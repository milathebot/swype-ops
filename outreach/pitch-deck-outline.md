## Slide 2: The Problem

Mobile DeFi is broken for normal people:
- Wallet popups on every trade → friction
- Order books → complexity
- 400ms+ confirmation → feels slow
- Web-first products on phone screens → wrong UX for the context

Result: average user opens a DeFi app once, gets confused, closes it.

**The gap:** there's no product that understands the "I have 30 seconds" context for trading on mobile.

---

## Slide 3: The Insight

**The average person checks their phone 96 times a day.**

Waiting for the subway. Pooping. Between meetings.

They're not going to open a terminal. They will swipe.

Swipe is the interaction model that fits the moment. Trading doesn't need to be complicated — it needs to be fast, legible, and done before you put your phone back in your pocket.

---

## Slide 4: The Product

**Swype — 3 swipes from idea to position.**

1. Card appears with trending coin + mini chart
2. Swipe right = long, swipe left = dismiss
3. Pick leverage + size → open
4. Watch live — chart, countdown, PnL
5. Auto-close at deadline

**Zero popup per trade.** Session keys handle it.

**Three tiers:**
- Chill: 10x leverage, 5-minute trades
- Degen: 50x leverage, 60-second trades  
- Retard: 100x leverage, 30-second trades

---

## Slide 5: The Tech

Built for Solana Seeker — Seed Vault wallet, Mobile Wallet Adapter, MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollups for sub-100ms execution.

Session key architecture: one wallet popup, zero popup per trade for 4 hours / 100 trades.

Non-custodial. On-chain. Real transactions.

**Target user:**

- Wants "the feeling" of trading without the overhead
- Uses phone in quick bursts — subway, bathroom, coffee line

**TAM:** Solana mobile users + casual crypto traders
**TAM adjacent:** general mobile trading apps (Robinhood comparison)
