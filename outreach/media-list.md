## Outreach rules

1. **Never cold email a reporter you've never engaged with** — find a reason to be on their radar first (reply to their tweets, cite their work, etc.)
2. **Launch day is the only news** — time-sensitive + visual (demo video) is the angle for crypto press
3. **Personal > brand** — you are the story, not Swype the company. "Fullstack dev who ships" is a better hook than "our startup"
4. **Keep it short** — if you can't describe it in two sentences in the subject line, it's too complicated
