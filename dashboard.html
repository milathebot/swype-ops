Competitive Position
Blue ocean — no direct competitor
confirmed

No swipe-to-trade leveraged perp app exists on Solana Mobile.

Key differentiators:

· Swipe UX (novel, shareable, screenshotable)

· Time-gated auto-close (unique narrative)

· Session key zero-popup (technical moat)


Key vulnerability: paper trading = demo, not product. Jupiter integration is the real unlock.

Target User

18–35, crypto-curious, not deep into DeFi.

Uses phone in quick bursts: subway, bathroom, coffee line.

Wants the feeling of trading without the overhead.


Launch angle: lead with the moment, not the finance.

"Done before your stop" — not "leverage perpetual swap".
